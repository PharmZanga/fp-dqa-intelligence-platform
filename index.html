Reproductive Health · Data Quality Assessment

Executive Summary

Strengthening the quality and use of Family Planning data for responsive planning, reliable reporting and accountable service delivery.

High-quality data is essential for effective planning, forecasting, procurement, distribution and monitoring of Family Planning commodities and services. Accurate and timely data enables health managers and policymakers to make informed decisions that support uninterrupted access to Family Planning commodities and improve reproductive health service-delivery outcomes.

The Ministry of Health has prioritized strengthening data quality and supply-chain performance as part of broader efforts to improve reproductive, maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health outcomes. Reliable data from source documents, the Electronic Logistics Management Information System (eLMIS), SmartCare Commodity Control (SCC) tools and the District Health Information System (DHIS2) are critical for monitoring commodity availability, quantifying future requirements, identifying service-utilization trends and supporting evidence-based decision-making.

To strengthen data quality and accountability in Family Planning commodity management and reporting, the Ministry of Health, through the National Supply Chain Coordinating Unit (NSCCU) and cooperating partners, conducted a Family Planning Data Quality Assessment (DQA) and Technical Support Supervision (TSS) exercise across selected facilities in Copperbelt, Eastern, Luapula, Northern, North-Western and Southern Provinces.

The exercise assessed the completeness, accuracy, consistency and timeliness of Family Planning data reported through source documents, DHIS2, eLMIS and SCC records. It also provided on-site mentorship and technical support to health workers responsible for data management, commodity management, reporting and the use of logistics information systems.
